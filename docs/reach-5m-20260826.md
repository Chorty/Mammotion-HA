# Reach is measured at 5.0 m — 2026-08-26

One supervised, explicitly authorized single vector segment on beta78 +
PyMammotion 0.8.12.post3, daylight, mower stationary off the dock inside
"Backyard Right". **Verdict: `target_reached` at 0.10148 m against a 0.15 m
tolerance.** The gate was armed for the run and verified disarmed immediately
after, from both the live API and RAW `core.config_entries`.

Evidence: `docs/evidence-reach-5m-beta78-20260826.json` (full 87-key record, 18
samples, 17 per-pulse diagnostics, both phases). Pre-dispatch dry run:
`docs/evidence-reach-5m-beta78-dryrun-20260826.json`.

## Profile identity is proven in fact

The dispatched payload matched all 19 keys of `docs/accepted-profile.json`
exactly, checked key-by-key against the response. This result is therefore
directly comparable to Gate 5 and to the 4 m reach run. No
`LUBA_ACCEPTANCE_PROFILE` key moved and no Gate 5 is owed.

⚠️ **Two traps that would have silently invalidated this test, both caught
before dispatch:**

1. **The CARD cannot run this test.** It auto-splits any leg over
   `SPLIT_LEG_TARGET_METRES` (3.85 m) into collinear sub-legs, so a 5 m click
   becomes 2 x 2.5 m and measures the splitter, not reach. The run must go
   through `raw_pymammotion_execute_vector_segment` directly, where
   `split_leg_target_length_m` defaults to `None` (off).
2. **The service schema defaults are NOT the accepted profile.**
   `max_linear_pulse_ceiling` defaults to `None` (accepted 22) — without it the
   run is fixed-budget at `max_linear_commands` and stops after ~1 pulse.
   `waypoint_tolerance` defaults to 0.08 (accepted 0.15) and
   `calibrated_forward_heading_offset_degrees` defaults to **116.5** where the
   accepted profile is **102.4**. A first dry run silently used 116.5. Send the
   profile verbatim; do not rely on defaults.

## What the per-pulse record shows

🔑 **The mid-leg aim divergence is real, and this is the best-resolved instance
on record.** Travel-direction error against the path grew monotonically across
the run — +0.59° at pulse 1, +2.34° by pulse 8, +6.47° by pulse 12, +12.39° by
pulse 14. This is the same "aim error develops mid-leg on straight runs"
behaviour recorded in 2026-08-10, now seen across 17 pulses instead of 3.

🔑 **The mid-drive re-aim fired at pulse 14 and it mattered.** Facing 284.91°
against a bearing-to-target of 266.876° gave an aim error of **-18.034°**; the
correction closed on `target_heading_reached`, and pulse 15 then travelled
0.3472 m — the LONGEST pulse of the run. Without that correction an 18° error
with ~0.74 m still to run would have landed well outside tolerance. This is the
first time the beta38/beta42 mid-drive correction has been observed firing on a
leg of this length.

⚠️ **Do not read the last two pulses as BLE stalls.** Pulses 16 and 17 travelled
0.0614 m and 0.1092 m, which looks like the stall signature, but they fired with
0.327 m and 0.218 m remaining — inside `final_approach_metres_per_pulse` (1.06),
so they are deliberately shortened final-approach pulses. Only pulse 10
(0.2369 m) is a genuine mid-run short pulse. Per-pulse travel otherwise sits at a
median of **0.3118 m**, min 0.2369, max 0.3472 excluding final approach.

## What this does and does not establish

✅ **Reach is measured at 5.0 m**, up from the previous 4.0 m demonstrated floor.
It stopped on tolerance with 5 pulses of ceiling headroom, so 5 m is again a
FLOOR, not a limit. The pre-dispatch cap `_MAX_SEGMENT_LENGTH_M` is 6.10 m and
the budget gate allows `22 x 0.30 = 6.60 m`, so 5.5 and 6.0 m are both
dispatchable and unmeasured.

✅ **Landing did not degrade with distance.** 4 m landed 0.1023 m; 5 m landed
0.1015 m. Flat, not growing. That is now the third distance (3, 4, 5 m) at which
measurement contradicts the pessimistic `_correctable_leg_length_limit_m` bound
of 0.58 m. **That constant stays** — it is a deliberately conservative advisory,
not a prediction, and it has never blocked a run.

⚠️ **n = 1 at this distance.** This is feasibility, not reliability, exactly as
3.0 m stood at 5 reached / 1 failed before it accumulated runs. Do not quote
0.101 m as an expected landing.

⚠️ **This says nothing about a 5 m leg AFTER a junction turn.** The leg began
aligned by construction. Reach and post-turn landing accuracy are different
properties and only reach is measured here — the same caveat the 4 m result
carries.
